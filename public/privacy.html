StockShadow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. What We Collect

  • Account data from your Google/GitHub sign-in: display name, email, profile photo.
  • Simulator data: your virtual portfolio, orders, trade history and KYC-quiz status.
  • No analytics/ad trackers: the app currently ships no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

2. What We Never Collect

No real financial data of any kind — the simulator cannot connect to brokers, banks or payment systems. We do not sell your data.

3. How It's Stored

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) tied to your sign-in user ID, protected by security rules that allow only you to read and write your own document.

4. What's Public

If your account is leaderboard-eligible, your display name, profile photo, and virtual net worth appear on the public leaderboard. You can opt out by using the in-game deposit feature (which makes your account unranked) or by contacting us.

5. Your Rights

  • Export / delete: email support@tradeflow-13116.web.app to request a copy or deletion of your data.
  • Reset: the in-app "Reset Simulator" wipes your portfolio and history.

6. Children

TradeFlow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
